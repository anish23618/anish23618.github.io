Collective Interaction Model

This kinematic framework models the self-organizing trajectory variations of three competing virtual species. The system demonstrates rapid structural phase transitions into cohesive, moving clusters via localized behavior vectors:

  • Stochastic Kick: Adds a vector perturbation in a randomized direction to evaluate system noise tolerance.
  • Intra-Species Synchronization: Aligns velocity with forward neighbors of the same group and applies an inverse repulsion vector if space collapses below a safety threshold.
  • Inter-Species Evasion: Implements distance-dependent probabilistic flip vectors and lateral blind-spot deviations upon checking foreign boundaries.
  • Boundary Soft-Potentials: Implements a smooth coordinates check that wraps or reflects agents gently away from the canvas border.
